Decide

A first read in seconds. A full report when it matters.

Stop spending evenings on applications that were never going to land. currnt tells you early, and shows its working.

  • Early assessment on every job. A score and a level (Weak, Consider, Pursue or Priority), with hard blockers like work rights listed up front.
  • Deep dive on demand. Three researchers look into the company, pay and practicalities. They never see your profile. One final step weighs what they found against your record.
  • No invented facts. A finding must cite a page the search returned or quote the ad word for word. Anything else is dropped.
  • UK visa sponsorship. Employers are checked against the Home Office register of licensed sponsors.

Write

One job, as many documents as it needs.

The Writing Desk sits inside each job. Keep a CV, a cover note and your interview prep side by side, each with its own history.

  • Markdown with a live preview, then export when it reads right.
  • Use the AI app you already pay for. Copy a CV prompt that carries the deep dive's strategy into ChatGPT or Claude, then paste the result back.
  • Drafts, not overwrites. A connected agent can propose a draft. Your live document only changes when you accept it.

For AI agents

Your career record, available to the AI tools you already use.

currnt runs an MCP server. Connect ChatGPT or Claude, and they can read your jobs and profile and draft a CV straight into a job's Writing Desk.

ChatGPT and Claude

Add currnt as a custom connector and sign in. You choose read-only access or permission to write drafts.

Claude Code

One command with a scoped token, or install the ready-made capture-job skill. A Hermes skill is available too.

Anywhere else

A capture API for scripts and a browser bookmarklet that saves the tab you're on.

Agents never see your contact details, and they can't accept their own drafts. That's always you.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do I need an AI key to start?

No. You can build your profile, track jobs and write in the Writing Desk without one. Add a key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google when you want deep dives, Coach or CV help.

Will an AI send applications for me?

No. currnt and connected agents can propose drafts. Nothing replaces your document until you accept it, and nothing is sent anywhere on your behalf.

Is currnt a job board?

No. There are no listings to browse and no employers paying for placement. Job hunt only checks the companies and boards you choose.

What do the AI researchers see?

Only the public details of the job. Your profile, goals and notes are never sent to the research step. One final step compares the research with your profile.

Does sponsorship checking work outside the UK?

Not yet. It uses the UK register of licensed sponsors and matches company names exactly, so it shows a match but can't prove a company isn't licensed.

Can I move my data out?

Documents export from the Writing Desk, and the whole app is open source, so you can run your own copy.
